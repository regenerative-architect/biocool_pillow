Overview

What this is: a next-generation sleep accessory concept. Start here.

BioCool Pillow™ keeps sleepers cool and healthy while contributing to a cleaner indoor environment. A plant-derived phase-change gel (PCG) absorbs body heat, and a mycelium-carbon air filter core passively removes allergens, particulates and trace toxins from the air near your face.

Evidence key. Claims on this page carry a label so you can see how far they have been checked: ● Stated by project as written by the project lead · ◐ Design target intended, testing planned · ○ Needs validation needs independent testing before anyone should rely on it.

Manufacturing & scalability

What it’s made of, what it costs, and how it could reach people.

Materials

Coconut- and soy-based phase-change gel · activated carbon and mycelium composite core · bamboo fibre outer cover.

Production

Can use existing bedding and gel-cooling product lines. Production cost $25–$50; retail $80–$150 per pillow. ● Stated by project

Scalability

Bedding retailers, eco-lifestyle brands and online direct-to-consumer stores. DAO-supported hubs can assemble region-specific versions from locally sourced materials.

Environmental impact

Market strategy

Who we’d serve and how it could reach them.

Target consumers

Sleep enthusiasts, allergy sufferers, eco-conscious shoppers and luxury bedding buyers.

Sales channels

Bedding and mattress retailers; online eco-lifestyle shops (Kickstarter, Shopify, Amazon); hospitality partnerships with eco-hotels and wellness resorts.

Selling points

Sleep cooler, breathe cleaner and save energy with one product; sustainable materials plus a refill system; eligible for green certifications and wellness branding.

Projected profit margin: 55–70%, with recurring revenue from refill kits and subscriptions. ● Stated by project

Fair-subscription principles (added). Plain pricing, one-click cancel, no countdown timers, no guilt-based messaging, and honest wording about what the pillow can and can’t do.
